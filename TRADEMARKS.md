## 1. Protected Marks

The names **"TontOS"**, the stylized logo, and associated visual identity assets are distinctive marks representing the official project maintained by FalseThinks.

Copyright licenses granted under `LICENSE-TONTOS-NC.md` **do not** include trademark rights.

## 2. Permitted Uses (Nominative Fair Use)

You **MAY**:
1. Accurately describe that a non-commercial fork or build is "Based on TontOS source code".
2. Link directly to the official repository (https://github.com/FalseThinks/TontOS).
3. Distribute unmodified community builds with original attribution intact.

## 3. Prohibited Uses

Without prior written permission from FalseThinks, you **MAY NOT**:
1. Name your fork, derivative project, or custom ROM **"TontOS Official"**, **"TontOS Pro"**, **"TontOS Edition"**, or any confusingly similar variation.
2. Use the official TontOS logo as the icon or branding of a modified distribution.
3. Market, promote, or sell commercial devices in a manner implying endorsement, affiliation, or certification by FalseThinks or TontOS.
4. Register or attempt to register domain names or trademarks containing the term "TontOS".
