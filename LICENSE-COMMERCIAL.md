## 1. Scope of Commercial Licensing

The TontOS operating system, native daemons, Android launcher, and system installation architectures are made available under the **TontOS Non-Commercial Source License 1.0 (TontOS-NCSL-1.0)** for personal, community, and non-commercial research use.

**Commercial licensing is required for all commercial installations, deployments, and commercial hardware distributions of TontOS (whether minimal, full, or configured with any or all companion applications from `apps/catalog.json`).**

## 2. Who Must Obtain a Commercial License?

A commercial license agreement must be executed prior to:
1. **Device Manufacturers & OEMs**: Flashing, pre-installing, or shipping TontOS (with any or all companion applications) on commercial smartphones, handhelds, or embedded hardware offered for sale or lease.
2. **Commercial Hardware Resellers & Refurbishers**: Flashing TontOS onto retail hardware and selling the bundled devices for profit.
3. **Commercial ROM Vendors & Distributors**: Charging fees, paywalls, or paid subscriptions for TontOS builds, custom firmware, or hosted update channels.
4. **Enterprise Fleet Integrators**: Deploying TontOS across corporate device fleets within commercial revenue-generating operations.
